# Asteroid Miner
A Space themed fast-paced and space explorable game where we use our spaceship to mine the rock, ice and rare metal containing asteroids and use the mined items to dock at our space station where we could upgrade our spaceship for more efficient mining and explore various nebulas and their stars and defend ourselves from blackholes, UFO's and meteors.
